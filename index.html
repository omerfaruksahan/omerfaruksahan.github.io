Read Fast

Read your own books, faster

Read Fast shows your PDFs, EPUBs and TXT files one word at a time in the middle of the screen. Your eyes stay still, so you read faster, with no training.

The page stays behind the word, so you never lose the thread. Bookmark pages, collect sentences into notes, and see a PDF page as printed when it has pictures or tables. Your books stay on your phone.
